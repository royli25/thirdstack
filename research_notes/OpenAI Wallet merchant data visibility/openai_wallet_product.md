## 1. Product name, launch date, countries, eligible users

### Takeaway
No product is called "OpenAI Wallet." The real product is **ChatGPT Wallet**, which OpenAI announced and began rolling out on **November 13, 2026** in a post titled "Introducing ChatGPT Wallet." It is a limited, gradual rollout: **US only**, for **ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18+** with **eligible US-issued credit cards**. At launch it works through **dots**, OpenAI's always-on personal agents.

## 2. What it can hold or pay with; stored balance; who holds funds

### Takeaway
ChatGPT Wallet stores **tokenized credit cards only**. It does **not** support debit cards, bank accounts, a stored or cash balance, stablecoins or crypto. OpenAI says it **does not hold customer funds or provide banking services** through the wallet. Money moves on ordinary card rails, from the card issuer to the merchant's acquirer.

### Cited Findings
- Only credit cards work for now; debit cards do not. OpenAI said it "does not hold customer funds or provide banking services through ChatGPT Wallet." — [Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/openais-chatgpt-wallet-lets-ai-154218980.html); [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/11/16/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-for-ai-agent-purchases/)
- Eligible cards are reported as US-issued American Express, Chase, Mastercard and Visa **credit** cards. — [Datastudios](https://www.datastudios.org/post/chatgpt-wallet-dots-agent-purchases-us-pro-users-visa-mastercard-amex); [Ghacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/11/15/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-so-agents-can-pay-without-seeing-your-card-details/) (search summaries)
- The wallet "does not hold crypto or a cash balance" and does not use USDC or any other stablecoin. — [Decrypt, "OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Wallet to Let AI Agents Shop—Without Crypto"](https://decrypt.co/383278/openai-chatgpt-wallet-ai-agents-shop-without-crypto)
- Other outlets agree that it excludes Bitcoin and stablecoins. — [KuCoin News](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-for-ai-agent-purchases-excludes-cryptocurrency); [Bloomingbit](https://en.bloomingbit.io/feed/news/124452); [OneSafe](https://www.onesafe.io/blog/openai-wallet-excludes-stablecoin-payments)
- Users keep their eligible card rewards and dispute or chargeback protections, because the purchase is a normal card transaction. — [Fintech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/digital-payments/openai-chatgpt-wallet-launch); [Ghacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/11/15/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-so-agents-can-pay-without-seeing-your-card-details/)

### Inferences
- There is no stored value and no omnibus account, so no third-party custodian is involved, whether a bank, Stripe, Coinbase, Circle, Bridge or Paxos. Funds flow as a normal card authorization from the user's issuer to the merchant's acquirer or PSP. OpenAI is not in the flow of funds.

### Gaps
- No source says whether debit cards, bank accounts or other networks such as Discover are on the roadmap.

## 3. Custodial or self-custody; per-user blockchain address?

### Takeaway
The question does not apply. ChatGPT Wallet is **not a crypto wallet**. There are no blockchain addresses, no keys and no pooled ledger balances. "Wallet" here means a card-on-file credential vault, closer to Apple Pay or Click to Pay than to a crypto wallet.

### Cited Findings
- OpenAI receives "a tokenized credential and limited card information," not the full card number or security code. This is from the Wallet Privacy Notice as summarized by CASRAI. — [CASRAI](https://casrai.org/news/openai-chatgpt-wallet-agent-payments-launch-2026)
- No crypto or cash balance. — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/383278/openai-chatgpt-wallet-ai-agents-shop-without-crypto)

### Inferences
- If any party "custodies" anything, OpenAI holds network tokens or credential references for each user, and the card networks run the token vaults. Neither holds user money.

### Gaps
- I did not read the privacy notice itself.

## 4. Partners and their roles

### Takeaway
The wallet was built with the **card networks American Express, Mastercard and Visa**. OpenAI's post also names **Chase**, but some outlets leave it out. The networks issue the payment tokens. OpenAI is also working with **PSPs Adyen, Checkout.com and Stripe**, and with **fraud vendors Forter, Riskified, Sardine and Signifyd**, so that merchant systems accept legitimate agent purchases. Stripe is **not** the core rail here, unlike in Instant Checkout. Coinbase, Circle, Shopify, PayPal, Tempo and Bridge are not named as wallet partners in anything I found.

### Cited Findings
- According to search summaries of OpenAI's post, OpenAI names American Express, Chase, Mastercard and Visa. TechCrunch and Dataconomy list only Amex, Mastercard and Visa. — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/chatgpts-new-wallet-lets-ai-agents-shop-and-check-out-for-you/); [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/11/16/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-for-ai-agent-purchases/)

## 5. Relation to Instant Checkout / ACP, agents and apps; autonomy and limits

### Takeaway
ChatGPT Wallet works **in a different way from Instant Checkout**. A dot uses its own browser to complete the purchase **on the merchant's own website and checkout**, paying with a network token. Merchants need no wallet-specific integration. Today **every purchase requires explicit user approval with a biometric check** (Face ID or similar), so agents **cannot spend on their own**. OpenAI says pre-approved delegation, within user-set spending, category and time limits, will come later, with no date given.

- **Future plans:** OpenAI plans to let users pre-approve purchases within spending limits, product categories or time periods, with biometric checks and records of authorization. No date has been given. — [Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/openais-chatgpt-wallet-lets-ai-154218980.html); [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/chatgpt-is-ready-to-reach-into-your-wallet-2000839784) (search summaries)

- **Background, 2025:** Instant Checkout launched in September 2025. - **Background, 2026:** OpenAI moved away from Instant Checkout around March 2026. Merchants now use their own checkout, and ChatGPT focuses on discovery. ### Inferences
- From the merchant's side, a wallet purchase looks like a **card-not-present web checkout** made by a browser agent with a network token. It is not an ACP or Instant Checkout API order. So the merchant stays merchant of record and owns the checkout, and it sees normal order and fulfillment data and a token rather than the PAN.
- I found no evidence that third-party apps in ChatGPT (the Apps SDK) can draw on the wallet.

### Gaps
- I found no primary confirmation of whether ACP or Shared Payment Tokens are used for any wallet flows.
- No per-transaction amount caps have been disclosed.
- The exact statement or descriptor shown on card statements is undocumented.

## 6. Any on-chain component?

### Takeaway
**None.** ChatGPT Wallet has no chain, no token and no on-chain settlement. Merchants are paid through ordinary card-network clearing and settlement. Stablecoin agent payments exist elsewhere in the market, but they are separate from OpenAI's product.

### Cited Findings
- No USDC or other stablecoin; the wallet runs on card rails. Decrypt contrasts it with Coinbase's x402 protocol, which typically uses USDC, and Circle's Agent Stack (launched May 2026) as separate stablecoin-based agent payment efforts. — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/383278/openai-chatgpt-wallet-ai-agents-shop-without-crypto)
- OpenAI has not said whether stablecoins or crypto will be added later. — [KuCoin News](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-for-ai-agent-purchases-excludes-cryptocurrency) (search summary)
- FinanceFeeds frames the launch as OpenAI entering an agentic-payments race against Google, Coinbase and Circle. — [FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/openai-enters-ai-payments-race-against-google-coinbase-and-circle/)

### Inferences
- If the user expects stablecoin balances, per-user addresses or on-chain merchant settlement, those features do not exist in ChatGPT Wallet as of 2026-11-20.

### Gaps
- No OpenAI statement on future crypto plans was found, and the search turned up no reliable reports of internal plans.

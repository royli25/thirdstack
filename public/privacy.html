Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Two Hands (“we”) runs twohands.tech, a tool router for AI agents and MCP hosts. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and password sign-in, or, if you sign in with Google, the name, email address and profile picture Google shares with us. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or any other Google data.
  • Tool activity. When your agent calls a tool through Two Hands, we record the request (for example a search query or a URL), which tool was chosen, the result, cost estimates and outcomes. This powers your activity history, usage and routing.
  • Settings and keys. Your routing preferences and the API keys you create (we store only what is needed to verify them).
  • Payments. If payments are enabled, Stripe processes them. We receive payment status and receipts, not your card number.
  • Technical data. Basic logs such as IP address and browser type, used for security and reliability.

How we use it

To run your account, route your agent's tool calls, show your activity and usage, prevent abuse, and improve the service. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Tool providers (such as Exa, Tavily and Firecrawl) receive the request your agent sends to their tool, so they can answer it.
  • Tool companies listed on Two Hands may see aggregate statistics about how their tool is used. These never include your raw queries, URLs, outputs or identity.
  • Service providers that host and run Two Hands: Supabase (database and sign-in), Render (hosting) and Stripe (payments).
  • When required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time by emailing us, and we will do so within 30 days, except where we must keep records by law.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and access is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant, we will let you know on the site or by email.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: support@twohands.tech

# Project instructions

- The product's visible brand is **Two Hands**, using `public/two-hands.svg` for the logo and `public/two-hands-mark.svg` for the favicon and compact mark. Existing `apilayer` MCP registrations and technical identifiers remain compatible.
- APILayer is a general-purpose tool router for free-flowing use in Claude, Codex, and other MCP hosts. Users determine the task; do not frame the product as a predetermined company-research or shopping workflow.
- The research conversation and planning belong to the external agent (Claude, Codex, or another MCP host). APILayer supplies tools and observes intent, explicit tool choices, evidence, delivered output, and evaluations.
- APILayer is backend infrastructure in the consumer's conversation. Record intent internally and continue researching automatically under server-managed authorization and spending policy. Never ask consumers to approve APILayer sessions, choose research budgets, or visit the operator dashboard. A shopping budget is a product constraint, separate from backend spending policy.
- Keep observed events, agent-reported explanations, and operator evaluations distinct. Never invent private reasoning, measured quality, verified model identity, insurance coverage, refunds, or paid-provider results.
- Use shadcn components where available; adapt them lightly when necessary. Use Geist, light grayscale surfaces, subtle pane shading, sentence case, and no eyebrow text. Use charcoal for standard filled actions and reserve blue for key actions and focus states. Give filled buttons a subtle top highlight, white labels, and existing corner rounding and control sizing. Keep content, charts, selected navigation, and the account trigger neutral. Use black for the logo and brand mark. Use an even grid layout and soft chips. Desktop sidebars remain expanded, with no workspace selector or navigation search; put Settings and Help in the upward-opening account popover.
- Always source interface icons from Untitled UI (`@untitledui/icons`). The shadcn generator may emit another icon package; replace those imports before keeping a generated component.
- The approved UI blue is **#3AA0ED**, from [Figma frame 71:2861](https://www.figma.com/design/WjofMoGOzEmDvoRdERi1O5/Untitled?node-id=71-2861). Always use `--brand-accent` for blue fills, links, and focus states; derive hover colors and tints from that token. The matching Figma button edge is **#318CDE** (`--brand-border`). Do not reintroduce older blue values or independent blue palettes.
- Blue buttons must keep the exact light blue `--brand-accent` fill and white labels, including hover and pressed states. Link-style blue buttons use the same unmodified accent. Roy explicitly rejected darker blue variants on November 14, 2026; do not darken fills or labels to resolve contrast checks. Preserve the approved edge and focus treatment, and report contrast results honestly without changing this design decision.
- All button labels must have no text shadows, including nested text, buttons rendered as links, and hover, focus, pressed, and disabled states. Do not restore text halos to work around contrast checks.
- When making Figma frames as iterations, never create frame captions; only name the frame.
- Keep provider credentials server-side. Commercial aggregate sharing is enabled for all observation sessions by workspace policy. Preserve historical consent events, exclude raw content from commercial exports, show company usage from the first eligible recorded activity without a workspace/session cohort gate, retain independent answer-review sample requirements, and do not automatically publish data.
- Preserve earlier research under `/research/`. Test fixtures must not enter the production workspace or provider/model statistics.
- Run `npm run check` and browser tests appropriate to changed behavior. Live provider checks require a bounded backend-authorized or operator-accepted session budget and must be reported separately from fixtures.

## Product knowledge base

- Use `research/README.md` as the index of saved product references and earlier research. Consult it and relevant notes for product, positioning, competitor, and provider-needs discussions.
- When asked to save an interesting link or idea, follow the capture workflow in `research/README.md`, use `research/_template.md`, and update the index. Keep source observations separate from interpretation and record a revisit trigger. Preserve earlier notes; a saved reference is not an adopted product decision.
